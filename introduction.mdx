EmPay is a Human Resource Management System (HRMS) for managing employee
operations through one role-aware application and REST API. It connects employee
records, attendance, leave, payroll, reporting, notifications, and administrative
controls so that each workflow uses the same workforce data.

Use EmPay to build or operate employee self-service portals, internal HR tools,
payroll workflows, and role-specific workforce dashboards.

## Why EmPay

Workforce processes often sit in separate tools, spreadsheets, and approval
channels. Employee details must be copied between systems. Attendance and leave
records reach payroll late. Staff cannot see the status of their requests, and
administrators have limited visibility into who changed sensitive information.

This fragmentation creates several recurring problems:

- **Repeated administration:** teams maintain the same employee information in
  multiple places.
- **Disconnected workflows:** attendance, leave, and salary data require manual
  reconciliation before payroll.
- **Unclear access:** employees and operational teams need different permissions,
  views, and actions.
- **Limited traceability:** sensitive changes and approval decisions are difficult
  to review without a shared audit history.
- **Slow reporting:** workforce information must be assembled before teams can
  understand attendance, leave usage, headcount, or payroll status.

EmPay brings these workflows into one system with shared records, role-based
access, and a consistent API.

## What EmPay provides

EmPay provides the application and API capabilities needed to manage common
workforce operations:

- **Employee administration:** create accounts, maintain profiles, organize
  employees, and manage company settings.
- **Role-based access:** assign administrator, HR officer, payroll officer, or
  employee permissions and present role-specific navigation and dashboards.
- **Attendance management:** record check-ins and check-outs, support re-check-in,
  and review personal or organization-level attendance history.
- **Leave management:** define leave types, allocate balances, submit requests,
  and approve or reject requests.
- **Payroll processing:** maintain salary structures, generate monthly pay runs,
  apply supported deductions, finalize payroll, and generate PDF payslips.
- **Operational visibility:** provide role-specific analytics, real-time
  notifications, email updates, search, and audit records for sensitive actions.

## How EmPay works

1. **Centralizes workforce records.** EmPay stores employee, attendance, leave,
   salary, payroll, notification, and company-setting data in PostgreSQL.
2. **Authenticates each request.** The REST API issues JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) and
   applies role-based access rules before protected operations run.
3. **Coordinates workforce workflows.** Separate API modules handle employee,
   attendance, leave, payroll, dashboard, notification, settings, search, and
   audit operations against shared data.
4. **Communicates changes.** Socket.IO delivers real-time notifications, while
   email workflows communicate account, leave, and password-reset events.
5. **Presents role-specific results.** The React application gives each role the
   actions, dashboards, and records relevant to its responsibilities.

## When to use EmPay

EmPay fits teams that need a connected system for recurring employee operations.
Typical uses include:

- Giving employees one place to check attendance, request leave, and retrieve
  payslips.
- Giving HR teams shared employee, attendance, leave, and workforce reporting
  workflows.
- Giving payroll teams controlled access to salary structures, pay runs,
  deductions, and payslips.
- Giving administrators role management, company settings, organizational
  dashboards, and audit visibility.
- Integrating selected HRMS operations into an internal portal or another
  business system through a REST API.

## Without EmPay vs. with EmPay

| Workflow             | Without a connected HRMS                             | With EmPay                                            |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Employee data        | Repeated across separate records and tools           | Maintained as shared workforce records                |
| Access               | Managed informally or separately in each workflow    | Controlled through four defined roles                 |
| Attendance and leave | Reconciled manually from disconnected records        | Available to related workflows in one system          |
| Payroll preparation  | Assembled from salary, attendance, and leave sources | Calculated from connected salary and workforce data   |
| Employee updates     | Communicated through separate channels               | Delivered through application and email notifications |
| Sensitive changes    | Difficult to trace consistently                      | Recorded through protected audit events               |
| Reporting            | Compiled manually for each operational team          | Presented through role-specific dashboards            |
